- Management Backoffice
- Planning Dashboard
- Administration Dashboard

- Personnel PWA

---

# DESIGN PHILOSOPHY

The Veele Services Platform is not a startup app.

It is not social.

It is not playful.

It is not consumer-focused.

It is a professional operational platform used daily by planners, managers, administrators, customers and personnel.

The design must communicate:

- reliability
- professionalism
- control
- trust
- efficiency
- operational clarity

Users must feel:

"We know exactly what is happening."

---

# DESIGN STYLE

Style:

Premium Operational SaaS

Comparable to:

- Monday.com
- ClickUp
- Asana
- Deel
- Rippling
- HubSpot
- ServiceNow

But cleaner and more operational.

The platform should feel like:

"A professional company command center."

---

# BRAND POSITIONING

Veele Services is:

- Security
- Cleaning
- Facility Services

The visual identity must represent:

- trust
- safety
- structure
- service

The platform should never feel cold or governmental.

It should feel:

Professional + Human.
