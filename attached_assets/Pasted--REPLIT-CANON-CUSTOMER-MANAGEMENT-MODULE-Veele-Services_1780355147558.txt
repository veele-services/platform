Version: 1.0

---

# PURPOSE

The Customer Management module is one of the most important foundations of the Veele Services Platform.

Customers are not simply records containing a name and phone number.

A customer becomes a complete operational environment containing all commercial, operational, financial and historical information related to that customer.

The goal is:

One customer profile.

One source of truth.

Everything connected.

No searching through WhatsApp.

No searching through emails.

No searching through Excel sheets.

Everything accessible from a single location.

---

# CUSTOMER AS ROOT ENTITY

A customer is the highest business entity within the platform.

Everything is connected to a customer:

Customer
→ Sectors
→ Objects
→ Assignments
→ Tasks
→ Personnel
→ Reports
→ Invoices
→ Payments
→ Documents

This allows Veele Services to immediately understand:

Who the customer is.

What services are provided.

What locations belong to the customer.

What work has been completed.

What is still open.

What has been invoiced.

What special agreements exist.
